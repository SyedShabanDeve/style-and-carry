Questions

Before you order

Is cash on delivery really available everywhere?

Yes — all major cities and most small towns across Pakistan. If your area is hard to reach, we'll tell you on WhatsApp before dispatching rather than after.

What are the delivery charges?

Delivery is free on orders over Rs 2,500. Below that, standard courier charges apply — we'll confirm the exact amount on WhatsApp before your order goes out.

Is this genuine leather?

These are high-grade PU leather wallets, not animal leather. That's deliberate — PU holds its shape, doesn't crack in Pakistani summers, and keeps the price where it is. Anyone selling you "genuine leather" at this price is not being straight with you.

Can I exchange it if I don't like it?

Yes. Message us within 7 days of delivery and we'll swap it for another style, as long as the wallet is unused and in its packaging.

How long does delivery take?

We dispatch within one working day. Delivery is usually 3–5 days depending on your city.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes — message us on WhatsApp with the quantity you need and we'll send bulk rates.
